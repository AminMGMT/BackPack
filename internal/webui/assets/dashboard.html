Add Backpack to your home screen Opens like an app — full screen, no address bar

Installed, the panel gets its own icon and opens full screen, straight to your tunnels.

  1. Tap Share in the browser bar.
  2. Scroll down and choose “Add to Home Screen”.
  3. Tap “Add” — Backpack appears with your other apps.

A browser only installs a page served over HTTPS, and this panel is on plain HTTP. Give it a certificate first — it takes one tap in Settings:

Settings → Panel access → Certificate

The panel then restarts at its new address, and this offer comes back:

https://

A self-signed certificate works on a bare IP; the browser warns once. With a domain, Let’s Encrypt issues a trusted one and no warning appears.
